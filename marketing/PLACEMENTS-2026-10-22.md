Research date: 2026-10-22 (Asia/Calcutta). Product: **Prarit Sidana PDF**.
Canonical URL: https://pdf.praritsidana.com/.

This report records public, primary-source research. Its author did not control
Chrome, create accounts, submit forms, send outreach or publish articles. Every
destination below is **researched, not submitted by this workstream**. The root
agent's authenticated browser evidence, if obtained, belongs in the SEO status
record and supersedes a researched form description. Zero new live backlinks
are established by this report.

The owner's current instruction resumes webmaster/account setup previously
deferred in the handover. Product accounts/billing, cloud integrations and email
signing remain outside application scope. Account permissions do not make
irrelevant bulk registration or fabricated reviews useful promotion.

## Required copy and truthful fields

Use the existing [launch kit](LAUNCH-KIT.md) for factual descriptions. Do not reuse
one announcement verbatim on every platform. Core fields for an actual listing:

- Name: **Prarit Sidana PDF**.
- Website: `https://pdf.praritsidana.com/`.
- Tagline: **Edit, sign and organize PDFs without signing up.**
- Platform: Web; document productivity / PDF tools.
- Actual features: 43 tools covering edit, visual sign, merge, split, compress,
  conversions, OCR, forms and page organization.
- Processing: final processing occurs on the hosting server; this is not an
  offline/browser-only app. Uploads/results expire after two hours.
- Terms: no signup for tools; file size, hourly task and resource limits apply.
  Do not represent usage-limited service as unrestricted unlimited use.
- Screenshots: current UI with generated sample documents, never private PDFs,
  signatures or identity documents. A logo is a product asset, not a maker portrait.
- Alternatives, if the form asks: Sejda, Smallpdf, iLovePDF and PDF24 Tools are
  relevant products to inspect/select. Do not assert parity or superiority.
- Source availability: a public GitHub repository does not by itself establish
  an OSI-approved software license. Inspect the repository license before
  selecting an open-source licensing label.

## Eligibility changes and destinations to skip

### AlternativeTo: current PDF-tool exclusion

The current [official FAQ](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) explicitly includes
PDF tools and suites of simple online tools among submissions it declines.
This materially changes the earlier launch kit's generic suggestion to submit.
Do not create an account or submit this app merely because competing established
products are already listed. The same FAQ explains that older similar listings
do not establish current eligibility, and forbids using user profiles as adverts.
Email verification and editorial review would also apply to eligible submissions.

### Google Business Profile: online-only app is ineligible

[Google's official eligibility rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en)
require in-person customer contact and explicitly exclude online-only businesses.
The repository establishes a web app, with no eligible customer-facing physical
business operation. Do not create a Maps/Business Profile by inventing a street
address, opening hours or service area. Search Console is the relevant Google
registration for this website.

### DEV: incompatible promotional AI use

[DEV's AI-assisted article rules](https://dev.to/guidelines-for-ai-assisted-articles-on-dev/)
prohibit AI-assisted business promotion and articles whose main purpose is
backlink building. Do not publish this campaign's launch/article copy there or
mislabel generated text to circumvent the rule. A future separately authored,
eligible technical contribution is a different task.

### BetaList: paid-only

[BetaList support](https://betalist.com/support) now says every submission
requires payment. No paid directory placement was authorized as a purchase and
this workstream makes no purchase. Do not spend time creating an account under
the assumption of a free submission.

### Fazier: reciprocal badge required for free submission

The [official submission page](https://fazier.com/submit) offers a free tier
requiring a homepage/footer backlink. Skip that tier in this campaign: adding a
reciprocal directory badge solely to manufacture backlinks does not improve the
PDF product. Paid tiers exist; no purchase is made or recommended here.

### Uneed: official pages conflict on free launch availability

The [official submission help](https://help.uneed.best/getting-started/how-to-submit-your-first-product)
describes free submission, account creation to save a product and a free waiting
line; it distinguishes saving a product from scheduling it. However, the
[official changelog, 2026-08-17](https://www.uneed.best/changelog/), says the free
line closed to new products and new launches require a paid option. The
[current launch guide](https://www.uneed.best/launch-guide) again advertises a free
line. This contradiction remains unresolved. The authenticated dashboard is the
final operational check. Stop at a paid gate; do not claim an immediate free live
listing from saving a draft or from marketing text.

## Article policy and natural writing

[Hashnode's code of conduct](https://hashnode.com/code-of-conduct) permits
AI-generated content when reviewed and taken responsibility for, but prohibits
bulk/automated posting, misleading links, SEO abuse and using the community
primarily for self-promotion. Contribute a useful engineering account,
with verifiable source/code examples and clear ownership of the product.

[Blogger's content policy](https://www.blogger.com/content-policy?hl=en-US)
prohibits unwanted automated, repetitive and promotional spam. Its policy page
does not provide a blanket AI-text exemption from those rules. One helpful,
clearly affiliated product blog is different from a network of replicated pages.
[Official setup instructions](https://support.google.com/blogger/answer/1623800?hl=en)
cover Google sign-in, blog name and available URL selection.

[Medium's AI policy](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/22576852947223-Artificial-Intelligence-AI-content-policy)
requires a disclosure when generated text is used; place it within the opening
two paragraphs. Keep the story free. Example: "This product guide was prepared
with AI assistance and checked against Prarit Sidana PDF's documented features."
Only claim checks that were actually performed. Do not invent a human editor,
personal experience, adoption figures or independent praise.

Natural prose means concrete steps, short sentences, real limitations and
examples. It does not mean concealing automation or impersonating independent
customers. Use contextual links where the reader needs the tool; avoid keyword
stuffing and repeated exact-match anchor text.

## Search expectations and evidence requirements

[Google's spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
cover link spam and scaled content made primarily to manipulate ranking. A small
number of relevant, useful placements is preferable to bulk accounts and thin
articles. [Google's generative-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)
emphasizes adding value; AI use alone does not establish quality or indexation.
No top ranking, recommendation inclusion, indexing time or referral traffic is
promised by a submission.

For each actual attempt record: destination; date; authenticated owner identity
without tokens; submitted product/story title; receipt or pending status; public
permalink when available; public read-back; and whether its product link works.
Do not store session URLs, OTPs, recovery codes or token-bearing screenshots.
Count distinct public referring domains separately from individual link pages.
Owner-controlled content is promotion, not independent editorial endorsement.
