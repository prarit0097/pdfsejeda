About

{{ app_name }} is a collection of online PDF tools: merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign and more. Each tool is a single page that does one job well.

How it works

You upload a file, pick options, and our servers process it. The result is ready to download within seconds for most documents. Files are deleted automatically {{ ttl_hours|round|int }} hours after processing.

Open technology

The service is built with Python and FastAPI on top of open-source PDF libraries such as qpdf (via pikepdf), PDFium, pdfplumber and ReportLab, plus PDF.js for previews in your browser.
